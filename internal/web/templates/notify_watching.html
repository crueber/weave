Watching

You hear about cards that are created, moved, closed or re-opened, commented on, or get a new due date. Watch a whole board with the bell next to the shortcuts button, a column from its menu, a card from its sheet. You never hear about your own changes, and one change notifies you once however many ways you watch it.
